Past & present

The situation ended.
The feeling kept going.

An experience can close and still leave something behind. Not as a memory you replay, but as a quiet setting inside you — one that keeps shaping the present long after the past has moved on.

The past does not stay in the past. It shows up in the present, wearing today’s clothes.

Where it quietly shows up

  • How you think
  • How you react
  • How you connect with others
  • How you see yourself
  • How deeply situations affect you

None of this means something is wrong with you. It means something in you is still asking to be understood.

Before you join

Questions, Answered

Is this session live?

Yes. It is a live online session, guided in real time. You are in the room with everyone else, not watching a recording.

How long is the session?

90 minutes, start to finish. We keep to the time so you can plan your evening around it.

Who can attend?

Anyone above 18 who feels something from the past is still shaping the present. You do not need a diagnosis or a crisis to be here — curiosity is enough.

Do I need previous knowledge?

None at all. Everything is explained in plain language as we go. You only need a quiet 90 minutes and an open mind.

Will I receive joining details?

Yes. Once your registration and payment are confirmed, the joining link and a short reminder are sent to you before the session begins.

Is this a replacement for therapy?

No. This is an educational and reflective session, not therapy or clinical treatment. It can help you understand yourself better and decide what kind of support, if any, you would like next. If you are in distress or need urgent care, please reach out to a qualified mental health professional.
